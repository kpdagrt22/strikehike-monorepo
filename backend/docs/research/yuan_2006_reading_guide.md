# Yuan (2006) Reading Guide

## Paper Access

**Title:** Are Investors Moonstruck? Lunar Phases and Stock Returns  
**Authors:** Kathy Yuan, Lu Zheng, Qiaoqiao Zhu  
**Journal:** Journal of Financial Economics, 2006

**Download Links:**
- LSE Repository: https://eprints.lse.ac.uk/
- Google Scholar: Search "Yuan Zheng Zhu 2006 lunar investors moonstruck"
- SSRN (if available)

---

## Reading Strategy (2-3 hours)

### Pass 1: Overview (30 min)
1. Read abstract carefully
2. Read introduction and conclusion
3. Skim methodology section
4. Look at all tables and figures

**Goal:** Understand the main finding and approach

### Pass 2: Deep Dive (60-90 min)
1. **Methodology Section**
   - How did they classify moon phases?
   - What time windows? (e.g., ±3 days around new/full moon)
   - What control variables did they use?
   - Sample period and markets?

2. **Results Section**
   - Effect size: What % difference in returns?
   - Statistical significance: p-values, t-statistics
   - Robustness: Did they test different specifications?
   - Which markets showed strongest effects?

3. **Discussion**
   - Their proposed explanation (investor mood, etc.)
   - Acknowledged limitations
   - Alternative explanations ruled out

### Pass 3: Critical Analysis (30 min)
1. What would you do differently?
2. What questions do they leave unanswered?
3. How can we extend this with planetary data?
